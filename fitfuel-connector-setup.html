Connect ChatGPT to FitFuel

Your FitFuel connector lets the Sunday task read recent training and save the same plan into your workout library.

  1. Install the private FitFuel plugin in ChatGPT.
  2. Choose Connect. FitFuel will open in a secure login-and-consent window.
  3. Sign in and select Allow ChatGPT.

FitFuel uses OAuth 2.1 with PKCE. Your password stays with FitFuel; ChatGPT receives only revocable workout read/write tokens.
